# Floating Spring-Dial Module · two-arm scene

One movable tabletop module: a red passive spring-return knob is mounted on the front of a thin white marked disk. The 8 mm disk starts on its rim. A slim 16 x 20 mm blue rectangular handle, 300 mm long, is attached off-center in the lower half of its back face. Its mass makes the free module tip under gravity until the distal end of the blue handle contacts the tabletop. The module is not welded or propped up; this motion comes from free-body dynamics and contact geometry.

The scene includes two Panda arms facing each other across the disk. The intended manipulation concept is one arm stabilizing the blue rear handle while the other grasps and turns the red knob. That behavior has not yet been tested. No task policy, dial-angle command, or LLM trial is included.
