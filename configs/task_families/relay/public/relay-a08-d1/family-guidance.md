# Air Relay

Read the assigned task's route, payload geometry and completion contract. Move
each assembly from its actual initial location toward GOAL; the START marking
does not require an initial return trip. Choose teams and handovers from current
robot capabilities and permitted observations.

Maintain the task's required real beam grasps and establish receiving support
before releasing a sender. Plan the receiver's access, sender's withdrawal and
the passive assembly's motion together. Different assemblies may progress
concurrently when full robot and payload paths permit it.

The final contract allows contact and stacking in GOAL, with the declared 50mm
boundary tolerance and 30mm low-release allowance. Check actual grounding,
release and containment; an opening command alone is not completion. Use the
task's process rules during drops and recovery, preserving observed failures.
